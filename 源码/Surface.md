# Floating desktop list

Mode: Operate. Native macOS, one compact desktop panel. User-pinned visual direction: recreate the supplied video; no independent style tournament is needed.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A quiet list remains beside the work, with no navigation journey required to see or finish a task.

OWN-WORLD: Translucent warm white panel, graphite text, small monospaced English labels, square checks, restrained native symbols, soft corners. Reference material is the video, rather than a generated visual comp.

STORY: Read the current list, check a task, change its state, or add the next one immediately. Editing and settings are secondary.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Approximately 340 × 420 points. A thin draggable top edge; four tabs; vertically stacked, collapsible lowercase categories; task text beside square checks and short due dates; quick entry at the bottom.

FORM: A single native floating panel, pinned by the user's supplied reference. Code-led app screen. No concept seed was needed for the fixed reference.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: Pointer entry restores readable glass; leaving softly fades the list. Compact mode can be enabled separately. Reduced Motion disables window animation.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
